# Phase 10.5 Plan 04: The structural guard invariant Summary

**The sessions route table is now asserted rather than reviewed: a recursive walker that refuses to pass on an empty list proves every route under `/sessions` carries an auth dependency, that `DELETE` carries the rate limiter, and that the three reads carry neither it nor the spend cap.**

## Accomplishments

- `api_routes(app)` walks `app.routes` recursively, descending into `_IncludedRouter` via `.original_router.routes` with `.include_context.prefix` composed onto the accumulated prefix. The `_IncludedRouter` import is guarded by `try/except ImportError` with an `()` fallback so the `isinstance` check is simply false on a FastAPI old enough to flatten. Its docstring records *why* it exists, not just what it does.
- `dependency_names(route)` returns `{getattr(d.call, "__name__", "") for d in route.dependant.dependencies}` — router-level and decorator-level dependencies alike.
- `AUTH_DEPENDENCIES = {"guard", "require_sessions_token"}` as a module constant, so the two ask routes (which carry `guard` and deliberately not `require_sessions_token`) count as guarded without a special case.
- `test_route_guard_invariant_over_the_sessions_tree` asserts non-vacuity **first** (`len(routes) >= 6`), then asserts the list of unguarded `(methods, path)` pairs is empty. Asserting on the list rather than a boolean means a failure names the offending route — confirmed by mutation, which printed all four.
- `test_delete_carries_the_rate_limiter` locates the `DELETE /sessions/{session_id}` route and asserts `check_rate_limit` is present, asserts it is absent from all three GETs, and asserts `check_daily_cap` is on none of the four. It carries its own non-vacuity check (`>= 4` routes, exactly 3 GETs).
- Both `-k` selectors that collected zero tests after wave 3 now collect exactly 1: `route_guard_invariant → 1`, `delete_carries_the_rate_limiter → 1`. Every automated row of `10.5-VALIDATION.md`'s Per-Task Verification Map now has a passing test.

## Decisions Made

- **One walker, not two.** Wave 2 added a private `_served_routes()` with its own recursive descent while fixing `test_every_advertised_endpoint_actually_exists`. Rather than add a second walker over the same version-specific FastAPI internal, `_served_routes()` now delegates to `api_routes()`. Checked before doing it: every endpoint the index advertises is an `APIRoute`, so narrowing the delegate from "any route with `.methods`" to "any `APIRoute`" loses nothing the caller asserts on — and that test still passes with its own `("GET", "/health")` non-vacuity check. `grep -c '_IncludedRouter'` is 4 (guarded import, `except`-branch assignment, the `isinstance` check, and the docstring mention), against the plan's floor of 3.
- **The threshold stays 6.** Verified by execution: the walker returns `/sessions`, `/sessions/{session_id}` **twice** (GET and DELETE), `/sessions/{session_id}/trace`, `/sessions/{session_id}/ask`, `/sessions/{session_id}/ask/stream` — six objects, five distinct paths. Lowering it to 5 would let a walker that silently drops one route still pass.
- **The invariant test takes no fixture.** It reads the app object, so it needs no client, no temp database, and no token. That also means it cannot be quietly weakened by a change to `make_client`.
- **`test_delete_carries_the_rate_limiter` excludes the ask routes by path (`"/ask" not in path`) rather than by dependency**, so the "exactly 3 GETs" assertion is a real count of the read group and does not silently absorb a future ask-shaped route.

## Deviations from Plan

### Improvements over the letter of the plan

**1. `_served_routes()` superseded rather than left in place**
- **Found during:** Task 1 (the plan's own context flagged it as a question)
- **Issue:** Two independent recursive walkers over `fastapi.routing._IncludedRouter` — a private, version-specific internal — is two places to fix on the next FastAPI upgrade, and two places that can drift out of agreement about what the app serves.
- **Fix:** `_served_routes()` reduced to a comprehension over `api_routes()`. Both its callers still pass.
- **Files modified:** `tests/test_service.py`
- **Commit:** `042e9e5`

**2. A non-vacuity assertion added to `test_delete_carries_the_rate_limiter` as well**
- The plan required one only in the invariant test. But this test's dict lookup would `KeyError` rather than pass on an empty walk, which is a confusing failure rather than an informative one — and the "no metered reads" and "no daily cap" assertions *would* pass vacuously on an empty walk. It now asserts `>= 4` session routes and exactly 3 GETs first.

No Rule 4 situations arose. No packages were installed.
