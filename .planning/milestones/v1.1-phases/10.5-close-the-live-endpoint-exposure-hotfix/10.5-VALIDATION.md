**Approval:** reconciled 2026-08-11 during the v1.1 milestone audit closure. Every row's evidence
was in the wave SUMMARYs at execution time; this file was never flipped, which is the audit's P1
finding. Three things this phase established that the rest of the milestone ran on:

- **A route walker that scans `app.routes` flatly is vacuous under FastAPI 0.141.1**, which leaves
  the included router's routes out of that list entirely. Wave 2 shipped one and wave 4 replaced it
  — `api_routes()` supersedes the private `_served_routes()` rather than sitting beside it, because
  two walkers over the same versioned internal is two things to fix on the next upgrade.
- **A structural test is not done until the walker has been replaced with the naive version and the
  non-vacuity assertion observed failing.**
- **The requirement stayed `Pending` through four of five waves, deliberately.** REQ-live-endpoint-
  exposure reads "not reachable without credentials **on the deployed service**"; until the v4
  cutover the hole was closed in code and still open in production. Marking it complete at wave 1
  would have recorded a fix that had not happened.
