# 17-04 Task 3 — the live closing check

**Status:** Complete — the reversal proven on the wire
**Date:** 2026-08-11 · **Fly release v11**

## What was run

Deployed from merged `main` (PR #14) — **release v11**, both machines healthy, `/`,
`/health`, `/demo`, `/metrics` all 200.

Then: one narrow research question, then a follow-up its notes could not possibly cover,
both over SSE with a shared cookie jar so the identity minted on the seed carried into the
follow-up.

- Seed: *"What is the HNSW index in pgvector?"* → session `6ef32aa61f404ed4b9e67fec0cec1361`
- Follow-up: *"What did the Reserve Bank of Australia set the cash rate to at its most
  recent meeting?"* — nothing in the pgvector notes touches Australian monetary policy.

## The node sequence — the whole phase in four events

```
responder    ← reads the notes, finds nothing, signals INSUFFICIENT
researcher   ← recalled_from_memory: 0, goes and searches
responder    ← drafts from the enlarged note set
critic       ← approved: True
```

Before this phase that sequence was unreachable: the supervisor ENDed with
`no_prior_research` and the responder's refusal text shipped as the answer.

## The trace — `no_prior_research` redefined, not dangling

```
supervisor → responder
supervisor → researcher   followup_research: "notes_insufficient"
supervisor → responder
supervisor → critic
supervisor → done
```

SC-4 satisfied on the wire: the reason is a **trace event** explaining why the follow-up
reached, not a terminal stop. `forced_stop_reason` is `''`.

## The answer — grounded, and honest about its own edge

`approved: True`, 578 chars, `cost_usd: 0.093745`:

> "Based on the research notes, the most recently **confirmed** RBA decision was at its June
> 2026 meeting … held the cash rate steady at **4.35%** … Note: there was reportedly a
> further meeting scheduled for August 10–11, 2026, but the research notes explicitly state
> that the outcome of that meeting could not be verified…"

This is the replacement guarantee working in both directions at once. It researched rather
than refusing (the reversal), it answered from the new notes (grounding preserved), **and it
declined to fill the remaining gap from parametric knowledge** — the model plainly knows
what an RBA meeting is and did not guess the outcome. That last clause is the guarantee
ADR-0011 claims, observed rather than asserted.

## Notes appended, not replaced

`/health` reports **8 notes across 7 sessions** after the check. The follow-up's research
pass added to the store rather than overwriting the session's existing notes — the
graph.py:333 REPLACE trap, closed and visible in production.

## Cost — over the ceiling, and why

| | |
|---|---|
| Planned | ~$0.35–0.45, ceiling $0.60 |
| Actual for the check | **~$0.85** across four runs |
| Live total after the check | $1.7155 |

Two of the four runs were wasted by orchestrator error, not by the system:

1. A research run completed server-side but the client `curl` timed out at 400s, so its
   `session_id` was never captured. The `run_finished` log line does not carry
   `session_id`, and `GET /sessions` needs the operator token — so the session was
   unaddressable.
2. A second seed was run without a cookie jar. The follow-up then returned
   `No session '1a5178…'` — **which is Phase 12 behaving exactly as designed**: the session
   belongs to the identity minted on the seed response, and a caller without that cookie
   gets a 404 byte-identical to a session that never existed. A correct 404, caused by a
   sloppy check.

The overrun was approved by the operator before the final two runs.

The estimate was also drawn before Phase 16's Opus critic flip and before research passes
were observed at 9 iterations; a research run now costs ~$0.25–0.32 rather than ~$0.18.
Worth carrying into any future live check.
