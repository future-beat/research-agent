      replay gate: a model-mismatch fire, a hand-edited fixture, a broken fixture, an
      orphaned fixture would all print FAIL but exit 0. So compute
      `replay_failures = [r for r in replay_results if r.error or not r.passed]`;
      the process exits 0 iff `summary["ok"]` AND `replay_failures` is empty. When
      replay_failures is non-empty, print each with its reason plus the line
      "replay is all-must-pass: a committed fixture was known-good at record time" and
      exit 1 even when the shared pass rate clears the gate. The behavioural denominator
      stays rate-governed at 0.9; the replay leg is all-must-pass.
    - Vacuous-replay guard (kept, and still needed alongside the exit rule — a silently
      skipped fixture produces NO CaseResult, so the all-must-pass rule alone cannot see
