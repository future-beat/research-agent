<objective>
Build the deterministic quality graders that will grade recorded answers: grounding
(risky-token containment), coverage (question-term overlap), structure (shape sanity),
refusal honesty, and per-case pins — each shipped with a passing AND a failing synthetic
fixture, each with its claim boundary in its docstring.

Purpose: this is what the suite is newly allowed to claim (the ADR-0009 core). A grader
that cannot fail is the seventeenth vacuous gate waiting; the paired pass/fail synthetic
states are the proof each rubric bites.
Output: five quality graders + two registries in evals/graders.py, pin fields on Case,
paired tests for every grader.
</objective>
