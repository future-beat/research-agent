<objective>
Close the injection hole the whole ownership story exists to fix: notes are written to one shared
store and recalled into OTHER visitors' runs, where the critic reads that untrusted text. Scope
notes to the caller identity in `add()` and `query()` across ALL FOUR backends — recall returns only
the caller's notes — and bound them with a 7-day TTL from `created_at`, matching session hygiene.
Thread the owner through the graph (`AgentState["owner"]`, `initial_state`/`followup_state`,
`researcher_node`) and from the service handlers (`request.state.identity`). The behavioural contract
suite (now 4 arms after Wave 0) proves the four backends behave identically — this is SC-5 made
literally true. `owner=""` is exact-match isolation (never "empty means all"); the two orphaned notes
match nobody and age out. Dedup was rejected (no identical cross-backend semantics); TTL is the bound.

Output: owner+TTL across json/memory/chroma/pgvector, owner threading through the graph, and the
4-arm scoping/TTL contract tests, plus a regression check that the walker and metrics invariants hold.
</objective>
