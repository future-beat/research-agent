- **The derived CSP still derives.** `index.html` has been edited twice since 19

  so it cannot go stale by construction. Proven rather than argued: the policy derived
  locally from today's file is **character-identical to the header
  `research-agent.fly.dev` served during this audit**, hashes and all —
  `sha256-RFbXKNVnBUevfNLt+zQFKptjx7o/xmwoskAAb8/yUIk=` for the script,

## What this milestone got right, worth keeping

- **Deriving beat freezing, and the proof arrived unrequested.** 19 chose to compute the CSP
  from the page's bytes rather than pin two literals, arguing that a frozen hash lets the
  served header disagree with the shipped page. Two later phases then edited that page, one
  of them a hotfix nobody planned. Under the rejected design the live demo's JavaScript
  would have been blocked in every browser with the suite still green.
- **Refusals were treated as output.** 21 could have bought the number forty by widening
  patterns. It shipped 19 recorded and 21 documented instead, with a union gate whose
  overlap arm then *forced* 21.5 to keep the record honest when six moved across.
- **A verifier's own gap became a gate the same day.** 21's retrospective verification found
  a promised check that was never written, demonstrated it by planting a stale judge and
  watching nothing fire, and the gate exists now. That is the audit chain working end to end.
