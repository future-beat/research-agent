# Phase 22.5 — Validation Strategy

> A hotfix whose defect the whole test suite missed. The suite proved the stream's
> contract and never asked whether a human would wait — so the gates here have to cover
> the *perceived* behaviour, not only the wire format, and the one thing that cannot be
> tested keylessly (a real 120-second gap) is named rather than hidden.
