runbook's recipe does, with a fresh --report path. Per outcome: a SUCCESS's
`REFUSALS.json` entry is deleted in the SAME commit that adds its fixture (the union
gate's overlap arm reds on the very next keyless push otherwise); a repeat REFUSAL's
entry is UPDATED to the new attempt's truth — kind and detail from this run's refusal,
cost_usd from this run's report, batch `"21.5-rerecord"` — never left describing the
Phase 21 attempt as if it were current. No retry inside the invocation, no
micro-checkpoint re-runs (CONTEXT ratified ONE re-attempt), no --force ever.
