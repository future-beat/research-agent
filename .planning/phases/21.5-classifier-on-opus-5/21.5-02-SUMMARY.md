## One test went stale by our own success

`test_cli_writes_the_report` pinned `len(report["cases"]) == 1` for `general-summary` — true
only while that case had no fixture. Recording it made the CLI correctly grade two legs
(behavioural + `@recorded`), and the literal went red overnight.

Fixed deliberately, not silenced: the assertion now derives its expectation from whether
the fixture exists, and asserts the *set* of graded ids rather than a count, so no future
record run can falsify it again. This is the third time this milestone that a count pinned
as a literal has gone stale behind a legitimate change — the pattern is worth naming.
